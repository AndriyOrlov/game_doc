## Спільний промпт

Use case: stylized-concept. One wide professional modular kitbash environment concept sheet for the original 3D game CHOIR OF EMPTY MACHINES. Attached reference is STYLE AND MATERIAL REFERENCE ONLY, not an edit target. Show 12 clearly separated complete reusable components on a clean matte warm-gray studio background. Orthographic three-quarter views, neutral studio light, generous spacing, consistent scale logic, clean hard-surface design, readable silhouettes, visible connector ends and repeatable junctions. Aged dark teal steel, ivory ceramic and restrained tarnished copper form the shared family. Small lower-right inset assembles a two- or three-tier passage from the modules. No characters, no game UI, no logos, no fused diorama as the main sheet, no exact engineering dimensions or claims. A short district heading and 'MODULAR ENVIRONMENT CONCEPT' subtitle only. These are original assets, not copied Stray or NieR content.

Inset: simple two-tier factory passage. Preserve original acoustic-industrial visual language, no junkyard clutter.
